Build Your First Skill

By the end, you have a skill that does one task you repeat often, tested on a new case.

How to Use This Page

The copy buttons copy exactly what you see in the box.

Before You Start

You work in the desktop app of your AI assistant, on a paid account.

Claude: download the app from claude.com/download. Open it, sign in and start a new chat. If it asks for a folder, create an empty one called "JCI".

ChatGPT: download the app from openai.com/chatgpt/download. Open it, sign in and choose Codex. If it asks for a folder, create an empty one called "JCI".

Your data: use real material from your work, but leave out what you would not send to an outside supplier: client names, personal data, passwords. Replace them with "Client A" or a rough number.

No business yet, or no material at hand? Use our demo business, Lutra Ceramics. It replaces only the material: you follow exactly the same steps.

Pick Your Skill

Choose the task you do often and want AI to take over.

At the end of this step you have: your skill picked and your material at hand.
2

The Interview

AI asks you at most 5 questions, then does the task once.

First pick your skill, at Step 1: your prompt appears in the box below.

New conversation → copy the prompt → answer the questions → AI does the task once

What you send: only the prompt below. AI asks for your material during the interview: then you copy or attach it.

Your prompt

      

      
How to answer the questions: answer the way you would brief a colleague who takes over this task while you are away for a year. Not too much detail, not too vague.

      
On the demo path: when AI asks, copy the matching part from the Lutra Ceramics file. For the topic or the client message, use the ones marked "for Step 2".

    

    
At the end of this step you have: a first result, made from your answers.

  

  


  

    
3
Check and Improve

    
Check the result and ask for fixes in the same conversation.

    
Your corrections in this conversation go into the skill. 3 checks, for the skill you picked:

    
Your 3 checks appear here after you pick your skill at Step 1.

    
  • Pick one number and check it against your data.
  • Find something you didn't give it: it should say "missing", not guess.
  • Read the 3 things to watch: each one should point to a number of yours.

    
  • Read the post out loud: would you say it like that?
  • Look for any fact you didn't give it.
  • Compare the length and the format with your 3 posts.

    
  • Check every price against your list.
  • Look for anything promised that you don't offer.
  • Read the tone: would you send it like that to this client?

    
  • Open the HTML file in your browser.
  • Check that only your colors and fonts appear.
  • Look for anything you said you never use.

    
If something is off, tell it in the same conversation what's wrong and ask for a new version. If everything is right, go on.

    
Example: "The post is too long and uses words I never use, like 'game-changer'. Make it half as long and keep my short sentences."

    
At the end of this step you have: a result you would use.

  

  


  

    
4
Save the Skill

    
Turn the conversation into a skill.

    
Copy this prompt into the same conversation where you checked the result:

    

      

        
The save prompt

        

      

      

    

    
Same conversation → copy the save prompt → AI saves the skill → it tells you how to call it

    
If the AI you're using doesn't save the skill, ask it how you can save it as a skill on your account and follow its instructions. Usually, you will need to download a file and upload it in Customize - Skills - Yours (Claude) or Plugins - Skills - Add (ChatGPT).

    
Where your skill goes:

    
    
      
  • Claude: Claude creates the skill and tells you where it saved it.
    • 
      
  • ChatGPT, in Codex: Codex saves the skill and tells you where it put it. The confirmation that it saved is not enough: the test in Step 5 shows whether it works.
    • 
    

    
At the end of this step you have: a saved skill you can reuse each time you need to do this task by simply asking for the task to be completed.

  

  


  

    
5
Test It

    
Use the skill on a new case, in a new conversation.

    
Open a new conversation and write, in your own words, what you want.

    
Example: "Use my business snapshot skill on this month's numbers."

    
The new case: next month's numbers, a new topic, a new client message or a new design. On the demo path, use the ones marked "for Step 5" in the Lutra Ceramics file.

    
What to watch:

    
    
      
  • It asks you only for what changes, not the whole interview again.
    • 
      
  • The result follows your rules without you repeating them.
    • 
      
  • What you corrected in Step 3 doesn't come back.
    • 
    

    
If something is off: go back to the conversation from Steps 2-4, tell it what to adjust and ask it to update the skill. Then test again in a new conversation.

    
At the end of this step you have: a tested skill you can rely on.

Share in Your Trio

    
Show your skill to 2 other participants.

    • 
      
  • Say what surprised you.
    • 
      
  • Ask the others: what would you add to it?
    •
